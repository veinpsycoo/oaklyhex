1. Summary

The App is a casual puzzle game for a general audience. There is no account, no sign-up and no social login. Your game progress is stored on your device. The App shows ads (Google AdMob) and offer optional in-app purchases (Google Play Billing). We do not use analytics services in the App, and we do not sell your personal information.

2. Information we and our partners process

a) Information stored only on your device

Game progress, settings (for example sound, vibration, language and the selected theme), level state, boosters, in-game gold and daily reward status are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes it.

b) Information processed by Google AdMob (advertising)

The App uses Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. When ads load or are shown, Google and its ad partners may collect and process, according to their own policies:

  • Advertising ID (such as the Android Advertising ID), and other device or app identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and software information (device model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type)
  • Information about your interaction with ads and the App (for example ad impressions and clicks, App usage related to ads)
  • Diagnostic and performance information about the ad SDK

This information is used to serve, measure and limit ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you allow it, to personalize ads. We do not receive your name, email address or similar identity data through AdMob.

c) Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. Payment details (card or other payment method) are handled by Google and are never received or stored by us. The App receives only a purchase confirmation (product and purchase token) so that it can unlock what you bought.

d) Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, precise location, or files. The App does not use user accounts, chat, user-generated content or multiplayer features. We do not use analytics tools in the App. If this changes, we will update this policy before the change is released.

3. Consent and ad personalization choices

  • EU, EEA and UK users: On first launch the App displays a consent form based on Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), in line with GDPR/ePrivacy requirements. You can accept, decline or manage your choices, and you can change your decision later with the "Ad Privacy Settings" button in the App's Settings, which appears where the form applies. If you do not consent to personalization, you may still see ads, but they will be non-personalized.
  • All users: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Opting out does not remove ads; it limits personalization.
  • Remove ads: The App offers a one-time "Remove Ads" in-app purchase that removes banner ads and the ad breaks between levels. Optional rewarded videos are shown only when you choose to watch them.

4. Legal bases (GDPR, where applicable)

Where the GDPR applies, our processing and our partners' processing relies on: (i) your consent for storing or accessing information on your device and for personalized advertising; (ii) performance of a contract (delivering the purchases you request); and (iii) our legitimate interest in operating the App, preventing fraud and measuring ad delivery in a privacy-conscious way, where consent is not required by law. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing.

5. Sharing and third parties

We share information only as follows: (i) with Google (AdMob and Google Play) as described above; (ii) where required by law or to protect rights and safety; (iii) in a business transfer such as a merger or sale, with notice. We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not knowingly share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising of users under 16 in violation of applicable law.

Third-party privacy information:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google AdMob uses data: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Third parties act under their own policies, and we do not control them.

6. International transfers

Google may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, transfers rely on recognized safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses, as described in Google's documentation.

7. Data retention

We do not keep a server-side copy of your game data, because it stays on your device. Data processed by Google AdMob and Google Play is retained according to Google's policies. Because the App has no accounts, we generally cannot identify you or look up data about you; if you contact us, we will use your message only to answer you and delete it when no longer needed.

8. Security

Local data is stored in the App's private storage on your device. Ad and billing communications are handled by Google SDKs over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads are served through Google AdMob in line with its policies. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an ad or purchase, contact us at oaklyhex@gmail.com and we will help get it removed. In countries where the digital age of consent is higher than 13 (for example 16 in parts of the EU), parents should supervise use and purchases.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR/UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA in California, and other state or national laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent, to opt out of sale/sharing for targeted advertising, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. We do not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Because we hold no account data and cannot identify you from the information on your device, most of these requests are met by the controls above (device ad settings, the consent form, clearing the App's data, uninstalling). For data held by Google, use Google's tools and the links in section 5. For anything else, email oaklyhex@gmail.com.

California notice: We do not "sell" personal information in exchange for money. Use of an advertising SDK may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law; you can opt out using your device ad settings or the in-App privacy options. Do-Not-Track: the App does not respond to DNT signals, as there is no web tracking by us.
